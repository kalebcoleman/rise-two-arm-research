# Asset and source provenance

Upstream revision identifiers and URLs are recorded in `upstream-lock.json`.
The local experiment builds on lab-supplied robot, gripper, quick-changer,
Vention CAD, environment and integration software. These were not authored by
the researcher. Their original licenses and applicable asset terms continue
to apply, including to geometry embedded in presentation USD files.

The integration checkout's MIT license is preserved at
`isaac-sim-mcp/LICENSE`. The upstream room is omitted from the portable
presentation scene. NVIDIA material files retain their supplied contents.
Isaac Sim itself and its runtime dependencies are installed separately.

This repository is initially private. Review upstream asset redistribution
terms before making it public or redistributing the asset collection further.
